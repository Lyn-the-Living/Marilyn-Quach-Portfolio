Marilyn Quach creates work to externalize and explore specific and meaningful recurring dreams. On the threshold of conscious experience and subconscious control are dreams, meditations, and nightmares. With her medical background, she investigates the phenomenon of hallucinations that occur while fainting. Her practices consist of layers of translucent oil paint, 3d modeled net art, and charcoal/pastel drawing. Through these diverse materials, she investigates the tandem balance of the subconscious and waking mind during the development of personal identity. She is particularly interested in how this self-awareness can be used to alter one’s perspective and combat negative coping mechanisms.
